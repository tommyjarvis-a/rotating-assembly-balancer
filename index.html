About

Rotating Assembly Balancer, an alpha tool: STEP import, static / couple balance, counterweight set-up, generation and check.

STEP export of the generated piece is faceted for now: planar triangles (FACETED_BREP of planar faces), not B-spline surfaces. NX imports it as a faceted or sewn solid. Each body is welded and checked closed before writing (with the hub, the hub is a closed solid too, or a clearly named reference surface if its mesh cannot be closed); read with plain OpenCascade its volume matches the in-app mesh to ~1e-14 (not yet checked in NX). STL export is the same triangles. Both are in mm, in absolute model coordinates, so the piece lands in place.

STL import: binary or ASCII, up to 256 MB / 5 M triangles, parsed in a worker; choose the STL units (default mm). An imported STL gets the Counterweight (existing) role and the added-material density, so a re-imported piece is checked in check mode.
